A LITTLE LETTER. A LIFELONG PROMISE.♡

My dear Malu,

Happy birthday.

I wanted to give you something you could come back to. Something that would still be here after the candles go out, the wishes become quiet, and another ordinary day begins. So I made this little place for you, with a few photographs and the words I sometimes struggle to say properly.

I am so glad you exist. I am so glad that, out of all the people and all the possibilities in this world, I get to know you. Looking at these photographs makes me wish I could keep little moments exactly as they are. But more than keeping the moments, I want to keep making room for you in my life.

You never have to earn my care by being happy, strong, or easy to understand.

You can come to me when your thoughts are a mess. You can say, “I don’t know how to explain it.” We can begin there. And if words feel too difficult, we can sit quietly for a while.

Whatever situation life brings, I will be beside you. When something wonderful happens, I want to hear every little detail. When something hurts, I want to listen without rushing you to feel better. When plans change, I want to help you find the next small step. I may not always know the answer, but I will keep showing up with patience, honesty, and care.

On your birthday, I wish you a life that feels kind to your heart. I hope you find people who notice your effort, days that leave you peaceful, and reasons to laugh when you least expect them. I hope the things you quietly wish for begin finding their way to you. And I hope you learn to look at yourself with the same tenderness I feel while writing this.

There will be new chapters neither of us can imagine yet. Through them, please carry this certainty with you: your place in my life matters. An ordinary message from you matters. How your day went matters. You matter, even on the days when you cannot quite feel it.

And that little Bangalore chapter we keep dreaming about? It can take its time. Some good things need a little patience, a few brave conversations, and apparently the approval process of a space mission. I’m ready with moral support, terrible jokes, and an unnecessarily detailed PowerPoint.

No rush, Malu. You don’t have to find all the answers today. We’ll take one small step at a time. My care for you doesn’t depend on a place, a plan, or how quickly anything works out. However that chapter unfolds, I will be here for you, just as I am now.

Happy birthday, Malu. I’m grateful for the moments already ours, and I’m looking forward to the ones we haven’t met yet.

I’ll be with you through the happy days, the difficult days, and all the life in between.

Always,
me. ♡

P.S. BANGALORE HAS BEEN WARNED.

And yes, I still remember our next New Year plan in Bangalore with Smirnoff. The very serious agenda: snacks, your laugh, and my world-class talent for talking absolute nonsense. We’re going to enjoy every little bit of this life together… even if Bangalore traffic keeps us in the same bit for three hours.

One plan. Two troublemakers. A whole lot of life left to enjoy. ♡
